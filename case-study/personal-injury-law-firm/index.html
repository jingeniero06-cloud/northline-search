The firm competes with large personal injury brands that have years of content and links. The goal was more qualified calls and form leads from organic search and the Map Pack, without paid ads.

The challenge

Starting point. Plenty of impressions but very few clicks (CTR 0.2%). Average position 28, on page three. Only about 75 pages indexed, and the Google Business Profile brought in almost no calls.

Search Console showed impressions spread thinly across hundreds of queries, with no page strong enough to win any of them. The site was relevant to many searches but competitive for none, and its city pages were thin or missing.

What we did

  1. Technical foundations

    Full technical audit and Core Web Vitals review, HTTPS and canonical fixes, XML sitemap review and resubmission, and conversion tracking for calls and form leads.

  2. City pages and Google Business Profile

    City pages for the markets the firm serves, starting with its home city, linked from an optimized Google Business Profile.

  3. A blog that answers real questions

    What to do after a car accident, average settlements, pain and suffering, PIP and no-fault, the statute of limitations, slip and fall, dog bites and brain injury claims. Each post links to the matching practice and city page.

  4. Written to be quoted by AI search

    Direct answers near the top of each page, FAQ sections, structured data and an llms.txt file.

  5. Speed held while the site grew

    Lighthouse stayed at SEO 100, Best Practices 100 and Performance 94 as new pages went live.

Takeaway

Key lessonStrong local pages and useful answers to real client questions moved the average position from page three to page two, with 100 keywords on page one. AI citations followed from the same work.
