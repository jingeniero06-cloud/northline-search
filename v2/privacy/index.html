Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026.

What we collect

When you submit the audit request form we receive your name, email address, website URL, selected package, approximate site size and any message you write. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this website.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your request, scope an audit and send you a quote. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which processes the submission on our behalf. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data such as IP addresses for security.

Audit data

Audits analyze publicly accessible pages of your website. If you grant read-only Search Console access, that data is used only for your audit and is not shared. Case studies are published only with client names and identifying URLs removed.

Retention and your rights

We keep request emails for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting engagement. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time.
