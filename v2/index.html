FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is included in a full SEO audit?

A crawl of every reachable URL, Lighthouse speed tests on key templates, schema validation, content and E-E-A-T scoring, local SEO checks, and an AI search readiness review covering AI crawler access, llms.txt and passage citability. You receive a PDF report, a prioritized action plan and CSV issue lists.

How long does an audit take?

Most audits are delivered within 5 to 10 business days, depending on site size. Sites with thousands of URLs are crawled in full rather than sampled.

What is AI search readiness (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization checks whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and other AI systems can crawl your site, understand your brand as an entity, and quote accurate, self-contained answers from your pages.

Do you need access to my CMS or Search Console?

No access is needed for the audit itself. Read-only Search Console access is optional and lets us prioritize fixes by real impressions and clicks.

Can you implement the fixes?

Yes. The Audit + Implementation and Ongoing SEO packages include hands-on fixes to templates, sitemaps, redirects, schema, performance and content.
