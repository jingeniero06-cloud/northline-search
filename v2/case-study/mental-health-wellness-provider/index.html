Case study · Mental health & wellness

From 20 indexed pages to 146 in three months.

A multi-location mental health and wellness provider with a large site that Google was mostly ignoring. Figures are from Google Search Console and rank tracking between May and August 2026.

Client name withheld for confidentiality. SEO program led by John Lester Ingeniero.

Starting point. Hundreds of URLs were published, but only about 20 were indexed in mid-May. Around 720 URLs sat in Search Console as not indexed, mostly "Crawled - currently not indexed", so most of the site's pages had no chance to rank.

Before and after

  • Pages indexed by Google~20 → 146
  • Rolling 30-day impressions~500K → ~700K
  • Keywords in the top 10~580 → 694
  • Keywords in the top 50~1,000 → 1,321

What we found

Google was crawling the pages but choosing not to index them. In health topics, Google needs clear signs of expertise and trust before it ranks a page. Blog bylines did not show clinical credentials, and the sitemap still needed to be submitted in Search Console.

What we did

1. Technical and Core Web Vitals audit

A full technical audit and a Core Web Vitals audit, a plugin audit, and QA of lead routing and tracking before changes went live.

2. Sitemap and Search Console

Connected the XML sitemap, submitted it in Search Console and tracked the indexing report as fixes went live.

3. Clinical author signals

Updated blog bylines to the provider's licensed clinical lead, so every article shows who reviewed it and their credentials. For mental health content, that is a key trust signal.

4. Keyword research and a content plan

Keyword research and a blog content strategy built around the provider's integrative approach to care, targeting the questions people search before booking.

5. Clearing the remaining indexing failures

Worked through the pages that failed Search Console validation as "Crawled - currently not indexed" until they passed. Unindexed URLs fell from about 720 toward 600, and technical errors stepped down in late July.
