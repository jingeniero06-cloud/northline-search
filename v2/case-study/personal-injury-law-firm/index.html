Case study · Personal injury law

Organic clicks nearly doubled in six months for a personal injury firm.

A Florida personal injury law firm serving several cities and clients statewide, in one of the most competitive local legal markets in the US. Figures are from Google Search Console, comparing February 18 to August 17, 2026 with the previous six months.

Client name withheld for confidentiality. SEO program led by John Lester Ingeniero.

Starting point. The site had plenty of impressions but very few clicks (CTR 0.2%). Its average position was 28, on page three. Only about 75 pages were indexed, and the Google Business Profile brought in almost no calls.

What we found

Search Console showed impressions spread thinly across hundreds of queries, with no page strong enough to win any of them. The site was relevant to many searches but competitive for none. Large firms with years of links held page one for the broad terms, and the firm's own city pages were thin or missing.

What we did

1. Technical foundations first

A full technical audit and Core Web Vitals review, HTTPS and canonical fixes, an XML sitemap review and resubmission in Search Console, and conversion tracking for phone calls and form leads. Indexed pages grew from about 75 to 227, and a July spike in unindexed URLs was pulled back down.

2. City pages linked from Google Business Profile

We built city pages for the markets the firm serves, starting with its home city, and linked that page from the optimized Google Business Profile. The home-city page went from 37 to 188 clicks. GBP calls went from about zero to a peak of 12 a month, with the Map Pack becoming a real intake channel.

3. A blog that answers the questions people ask before they call

Posts on what to do after a car accident, average settlements, pain and suffering, PIP and no-fault insurance, the statute of limitations, slip and fall claims, dog bites, brain injury settlements and filing without a lawyer. Each one links to the matching practice page and city page, with a clear call to action.

4. Written to be quoted by AI search

Direct answers near the top of each page, FAQ sections, structured data and an llms.txt file. 61 pages were cited across Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT and Gemini.

5. Speed held while the site grew

Lighthouse scores stayed at SEO 100, Best Practices 100 and Performance 94 as new pages went live.
