What we found

  1. Pricing placeholders and contradicting fees

    The live fees page showed "Confirm before publish" in four cells, and renewal and management fees on city pages disagreed with it. AI assistants quote this text directly to prospects.

  2. A broken phone link on every page

    The footer call button pointed to a web address instead of a tel: link, so every mobile tap on all 2,120 pages went to an error page.

  3. Mobile speed: 15.1-second LCP

    A caching plugin lazy-loaded the hero image, and three Google tag libraries (~530 KB) plus reCAPTCHA (356 KB) loaded on every page.

  4. AI crawlers blocked at the origin

    Uncached URLs, including every blog post, llms.txt and the sitemaps, returned 403 to a major AI crawler.

  5. Out-of-date legal content

    The Maryland landlord-tenant guide still described a security-deposit limit that changed in October 2024.

  6. Index hygiene

    Redirecting and noindex URLs in the sitemap, an indexable theme demo post and duplicate pages, two county contact buttons returning 404, and internal links carrying UTM parameters.

Takeaway

Key lessonMost of the highest-impact problems were cheap template and configuration fixes, invisible to a sampled report and obvious to a full crawl with live re-checks.
