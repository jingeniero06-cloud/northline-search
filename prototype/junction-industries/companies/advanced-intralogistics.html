Maximizing Real Estate Density with AVSRS Automation

Real estate density is the primary financial constraint facing modern commercial and mixed-use urban developments. Advanced Intralogistics engineers and deploys turnkey Automated Vehicle Storage and Retrieval Systems (AVSRS) and high-velocity material handling conveyors that eliminate conventional parking ramps, drive aisles, and passenger elevators.

By utilizing automated palletized shuttle conveyors, laser-guided positioning, and industrial SCADA software, our systems park up to 2.5x more vehicles within the exact same cubic footprint of a traditional concrete parking garage, delivering sub-90 second retrieval times.
