Engineering, Procurement & Turnkey Construction

Trident Structures delivers end-to-end industrial facilities engineered for heavy duty cycle durability. From bulk cement storage silos and aggregate blending plants to high-throughput railcar loading terminals, our engineering division handles every phase: geotechnical coordination, drilled pier foundations, structural steel framing, mechanical conveying, and automation controls.

In addition, Trident Structures is one of just a handful of companies in the United States providing end-to-end High-Density Parking solutions, designed to help commercial property owners maximize real estate density with automated asset storage and retrieval systems.

Single-Source EPC Accountability
Because Trident Structures integrates directly with Harbor Fab for structural steel fabrication and Junction's specialized foundation drilling crews, our clients eliminate multi-tiered subcontract markups and finger-pointing delays.

Standard Execution Methodology

Every Trident Structures project follows a rigorous 4-stage engineering lifecycle:

  • 1. Front-End Engineering Design (FEED): Material flow simulation, site geotechnical review, structural load modeling, and permitting.
  • 2. Foundation & Civil Execution: Subsurface drilled shaft piers, heavily reinforced grade beams, and mass concrete foundation slabs.
  • 3. Structural Steel & Silo Erection: Precision rigging of prefabricated vessel silos, aggregate bins, catwalks, and transfer towers.
  • 4. Electrical SCADA & Commissioning: Industrial PLC automation, load cell calibration, dust collector certification, and operational turnover.
