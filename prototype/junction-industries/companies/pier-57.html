Well-Located. Well-Built. Well-Leased.

Pier 57 bridges the gap between physical civil engineering and institutional real estate asset creation. As a wholly-owned development entity within Junction Industries, Pier 57 originates, plans, and builds commercial, industrial, and specialized infrastructure assets that leverage our internal engineering and construction capabilities.

Core Development Focus Areas

Asset Category Development Thesis Junction Strategic Synergy
Automated Parking Real Estate High-density urban parking assets integrated into dense commercial hubs. Direct engineering and hardware supply from Advanced Intralogistics & Trident.
Industrial Storage & Transload Yards Heavy axle logistics facilities, rail-served industrial depots, equipment laydown yards. Site civil engineering, drilled pier foundations, and Harbor Fab steel framing.
Specialized Marine & Waterfront Commercial waterfront infrastructure, heavy-duty piling, and floating dock systems. Foundation drilling, heavy structural steel fabrication, and marine engineering.
The Development Synergy
Because Pier 57 develops projects using Junction's captive engineering and construction subsidiaries, projects benefit from compressed construction timelines, lower cost basis, and superior long-term asset quality.
