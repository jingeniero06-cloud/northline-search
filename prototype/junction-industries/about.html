We Are Junction Industries

Junction Industries is a veteran-owned business providing foundational support for growing companies. More than just a Holding Company of infrastructure, technology, and industrial subsidiaries, we seek synergies where resources, knowledge, and know-how can be pooled. By doing so, we not only deliver cost savings to our portfolio of companies but a shared sense of purpose, passion, and personal growth.

Headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas, at 3348 Peden Road, Suite 600, Junction Industries deploys capital, operational discipline, and technical leadership across heavy industrial design-build EPC, commercial steel fabrication, material handling automation, and heavy civil construction.

Our People
Regardless of where they are in their careers, our people are hungry, humble, and smart. Combined, our companies employ hundreds of people; we encourage each individual's personal and professional growth while providing safe and supportive work environments dedicated to getting them home safe each night.
Our Passion
To help maintain our edge in a highly competitive world, we seek and reward creativity. Top to bottom, we welcome input and new ideas. This helps cultivate a shared passion and excitement in the people, projects, and processes that drive our success.
Our Purpose
Our mission is building great companies and developing extraordinary leaders that honor God through clarity of purpose, passion, people, and process. While each of our companies stands and excels on their own, they share a common focus—delivering exceptional expertise and customer service.

Institutional Strength & Credentials

By offering a broad range of complementing services, we set the stage for growing stronger together with each successful job completed. From heavy civil deep pier foundation drilling to turnkey concrete batch plant erection and high-density automated parking, our entities operate with absolute accountability.
