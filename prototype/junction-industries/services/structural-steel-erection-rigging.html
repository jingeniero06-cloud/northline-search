70,000+ Square Feet of Fabrication Backed by Certified Field Rigging

Through Harbor Fab, Junction Industries operates over 70,000 square feet of combined workshop space across three regional Texas shops. We fabricate and erect heavy structural framing, industrial ductwork, pressure vessels, equipment skids, catwalks, and miscellaneous metals.

Our field crews specialize in complex, heavy crane rigging within active operating plants, ensuring zero unplanned downtime, strict OSHA compliance, and exact anchor bolt alignment.

Junction Group Single-Source Delivery
By integrating engineering design, captive structural steel fabrication (Harbor Fab), deep civil drilling, and equipment erection, Junction Industries eliminates the traditional delays and cost overruns caused by fragmented subcontracting.

Execution Methodology

  • 1. 3D BIM & Detailing: Tekla structural modeling and CNC DSTV export for exact automated beam processing.
  • 2. Shop Fabrication & CWI Inspection: High-capacity CNC plasma cutting, submerged arc welding, and Certified Welding Inspection.
  • 3. Surface Treatment & Logistics: Commercial blast cleaning (SSPC-SP6), primer coating, and sequenced flatbed delivery.
  • 4. Crane Rigging & Bolt-Up: Engineered lift execution, plumb-and-align surveying, and high-strength tensioning.
