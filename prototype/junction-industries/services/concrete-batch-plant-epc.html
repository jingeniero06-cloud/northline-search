Turnkey EPC Delivery for Ready-Mix & Precast Production

Through Trident Structures, Junction Industries delivers complete turnkey Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) solutions for industrial concrete batch plants. We engineer facilities designed to withstand continuous 24/7 duty cycles while maintaining exact mix tolerances.

From initial site layout and foundation civil work to steel fabrication, aggregate bin construction, pneumatic piping, and SCADA automation, our single-source model guarantees seamless operational turnover.

Junction Group Single-Source Delivery
By integrating engineering design, captive structural steel fabrication (Harbor Fab), deep civil drilling, and equipment erection, Junction Industries eliminates the traditional delays and cost overruns caused by fragmented subcontracting.

Execution Methodology

  • 1. Conceptual Modeling & Sizing: Yard flow simulation, truck turnaround optimization, and environmental permitting.
  • 2. Foundation & Civil Execution: Drilled pier foundations, aggregate ramp retaining walls, and washout reclaim pits.
  • 3. Structural Steel & Vessel Erection: Harbor Fab steel erection, silo tower lifting, and aggregate feed conveyors.
  • 4. Calibration & Mix Validation: Precision scale calibration, pneumatic dust filtration certification, and commercial batch validation.
