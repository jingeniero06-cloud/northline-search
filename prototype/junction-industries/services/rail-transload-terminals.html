Rail Transload Terminals & Bulk Silo Systems

Turnkey engineering and construction of industrial rail transload facilities, under-track railcar unloaders, bulk storage silos, and truck loadouts.

Request Commercial RFQ / Bid All Services Directory

High-Throughput Railcar Receiving & Distribution Facilities

Industrial bulk commodities—including cement, fly ash, lime, frac sand, and grain—require robust, high-volume transfer facilities. Junction Industries engineers and builds unit-train rail transload terminals that minimize railcar dwell times and maximize logistics throughput.

Our rail projects encompass track design coordination with BNSF and Union Pacific, under-track pit hopper excavation, high-capacity bucket elevators, pneumatic dense-phase conveyance, and automated truck loading lanes.

Junction Group Single-Source Delivery
By integrating engineering design, captive structural steel fabrication (Harbor Fab), deep civil drilling, and equipment erection, Junction Industries eliminates the traditional delays and cost overruns caused by fragmented subcontracting.

Execution Methodology

  • 1. Class I Rail Interface: Civil grading, turnouts, curvature radii, and signaling aligned with railroad design criteria.
  • 2. Track Pit & Civil Excavation: Heavily reinforced subgrade track pit construction with high-capacity sump systems.
  • 3. Silo & Tower Erection: Prefabricated vessel lifting, high-angle enclosed conveyors, and pneumatic transfer blowers.
  • 4. Automated Loadout Commissioning: RFID automated ticketing, dust collection compliance, and high-velocity truck cycles.
