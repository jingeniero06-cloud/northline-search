Rail Transload

Rail Car Transload Terminal Engineering & Dry Bulk Logistics

Junction Industries' operating entities engineer, fabricate, and erect high-speed rail car transload facilities designed to transfer dry bulk commodities—cement, fly ash, sand, aggregates, and agricultural grains—between railcars, storage silos, and pneumatic bulk trucks.

Our terminals incorporate under-track car unloaders, enclosed drag chain conveyors, high-capacity bucket elevators, and automated truck load-out scale systems to maximize car turnaround times and eliminate demurrage charges.

Class I Railroad Interconnect Compliance

We engineer all transload layouts to meet exact track clearance, safety interlocks, and design standards required by BNSF, Union Pacific, and regional short-line railroad authorities.
