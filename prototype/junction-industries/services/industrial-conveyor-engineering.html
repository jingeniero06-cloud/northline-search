High-Velocity Bulk Material & Automated Storage Systems

Through Advanced Intralogistics and Trident Structures, Junction Industries engineers continuous conveyance systems for both heavy industrial bulk commodities and high-density automated vehicle storage.

Our conveyor systems are engineered for continuous mechanical reliability, featuring heavy-duty CEMA standard idlers, high-tensile vulcanized belting, variable frequency drives (VFD), and integrated laser alignment.

Junction Group Single-Source Delivery
By integrating engineering design, captive structural steel fabrication (Harbor Fab), deep civil drilling, and equipment erection, Junction Industries eliminates the traditional delays and cost overruns caused by fragmented subcontracting.

Execution Methodology

  • 1. Material Trajectory & Dynamic Simulation: Discrete Element Method (DEM) flow analysis to prevent chute bottlenecks.
  • 2. Truss & Gallery Fabrication: Enclosed steel gallery frames fabricated at Harbor Fab with full safety walkway access.
  • 3. Drive & Belting Installation: High-efficiency gearmotors, vulcanized continuous belt splicing, and self-cleaning take-up pulleys.
  • 4. Controls & Zero-Speed Interlocks: PLC safety interlocking, pull-cord emergency stops, and temperature sensor integration.
