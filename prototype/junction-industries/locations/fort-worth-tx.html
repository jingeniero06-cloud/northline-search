Corporate Headquarters & Industrial Operations in Fort Worth

As our corporate headquarters, Fort Worth serves as the operational brain and heavy equipment dispatch center for Junction Industries. Located at 3348 Peden Road, Suite 600, Fort Worth, TX 76179, our executive team oversees turnkey EPC delivery, structural steel fabrication, and deep foundation drilling across North Texas.

Our local operating entities—including Trident Structures, Harbor Fab, Advanced Intralogistics, and Pier 57—maintain centralized coordination with local rail interchanges, material aggregate quarries, and regional industrial parks.

Regional Transit & Freight Corridors
Serving the complete I-35W, I-820, Hwy 287, and Alliance corridors with direct rail interchange connecting to BNSF and Union Pacific North Texas rail yards.
