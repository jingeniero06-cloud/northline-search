Civil Foundations & Industrial EPC along the I-35 Corridor

The Austin-San Antonio corridor is one of the fastest growing industrial and urban regions in North America. Junction Industries provides turnkey concrete batch plants, automated high-density parking systems, and deep foundation drilling throughout Travis, Bexar, Hays, and Comal counties.

Our drilling division is uniquely experienced with Central Texas geology, utilizing core barrels and rock augers to seat high-capacity drilled shafts directly into competent Edwards and Glen Rose limestone formations.
