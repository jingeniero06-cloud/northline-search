Heavy Industrial & Marine Infrastructure in Greater Houston

Greater Houston and the Texas Gulf Coast represent the nation's premier petrochemical and maritime transload corridor. Junction Industries delivers heavy structural steel fabrication, high-capacity marine piling, rail transload terminals, and concrete foundations engineered for coastal conditions.

Backed by Harbor Fab's 70,000+ square feet of Texas fabrication space, we fabricate heavy pipe bridges, industrial catwalks, equipment skids, and API/AISC certified structures tailored for the energy and shipping industries.

Regional Transit & Freight Corridors
Serving the I-10, I-45, Hwy 225, and Beltway 8 corridors across the Houston Ship Channel, Baytown, Pasadena, Freeport, and Beaumont/Port Arthur.
